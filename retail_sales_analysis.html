Retail Sales Performance Analysis

This project analyzes retail sales data using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib. The goal is to understand total revenue, order performance, customer behavior, product category performance, monthly sales trends and business growth opportunities.

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail transaction data to understand sales performance, customer behavior and product category contribution.

The analysis includes data cleaning, feature engineering, KPI creation, sales trend analysis, category-wise performance analysis and final business recommendations.

Business Problem

The main business problem is to understand which product categories, customer segments and time periods generate the highest revenue. This helps the business improve sales strategy, customer targeting and product planning.

Key KPIs

Total Revenue

Total sales amount generated from all transactions.

Total Orders

Total number of unique customer transactions.

Total Customers

Total number of unique customers in the dataset.

Average Order Value

Average amount spent by customers per order.

Tools Used

Python Pandas Matplotlib Jupyter Notebook

Analysis Performed

  • Loaded and explored retail sales data using Pandas.
  • Checked dataset rows, columns, data types and basic summary.
  • Cleaned column names for easier analysis.
  • Checked missing values and duplicate records.
  • Converted date column into proper datetime format.
  • Created year, month, month name and day name columns.
  • Created customer age groups for segmentation.
  • Calculated total revenue, total orders, total customers and average order value.
  • Analyzed revenue by product category, gender, age group and month.
  • Created visualizations using Matplotlib.

Business Questions Answered

  • What is the total revenue generated by the business?
  • How many total orders were placed?
  • Which product category generated the highest revenue?
  • Which product category had the highest quantity sold?
  • Which gender contributed more to sales?
  • Which age group generated the highest revenue?
  • What is the monthly sales trend?
  • Which day of the week generated stronger sales?

Visualizations Created

  • Revenue by Product Category
  • Quantity Sold by Product Category
  • Gender-wise Revenue
  • Revenue by Age Group
  • Monthly Sales Trend
  • Revenue by Day of Week
  • Category-wise Revenue by Gender

Key Insights

  • The highest revenue-generating product category contributes the most to total sales.
  • Customer age group analysis helps identify the most valuable customer segment.
  • Monthly sales trend helps identify high-performing and low-performing months.
  • Gender-wise analysis helps understand customer purchasing behavior.
  • Average Order Value helps understand customer spending behavior per transaction.

Business Recommendations

  • Focus marketing campaigns on the highest revenue-generating product category.
  • Create targeted offers for the most valuable age group.
  • Improve sales strategy during low-performing months.
  • Promote high average order value categories to increase revenue.
  • Use customer segmentation to improve product recommendations.

Project Conclusion

This project demonstrates practical Data Analyst skills including data cleaning, feature engineering, KPI creation, grouping, aggregation, visualization and business insight generation using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib.
