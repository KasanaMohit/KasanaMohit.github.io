Customer Churn Analysis

This project analyzes customer churn data using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib. The goal is to identify churned customers, churn rate, high-risk customer segments, contract impact, payment method impact, tenure behavior and monthly charges patterns.

Project Overview

Customer churn means customers leaving a service or company. This project analyzes customer data to understand which factors are linked with customer churn.

The analysis focuses on churn rate, customer tenure, monthly charges, contract type, payment method and high-risk customer segments.

Business Problem

The main business problem is to understand why customers are leaving the company and which customer segments are at higher risk of churn. This helps the company improve retention strategy and reduce revenue loss.

Key KPIs

Total Customers

Total number of customers available in the dataset.

Churned Customers

Total customers who left the company.

Churn Rate

Percentage of customers who churned.

Average Monthly Charges

Average monthly amount charged to customers.

Python Pandas Matplotlib Jupyter Notebook

Key Insights

  • Month-to-month contract customers are more likely to churn.
  • Customers with low tenure show higher churn risk.
  • Electronic check payment method is linked with higher churn.
  • Customers with higher monthly charges may have higher churn probability.
  • Contract type, tenure and payment method are important churn indicators.

Business Recommendations

  • Focus retention campaigns on month-to-month contract customers.
  • Give onboarding support and offers to new customers with low tenure.
  • Review payment experience for electronic check users.
  • Offer discounts or better value plans to high monthly charge customers.
  • Create customer risk segments to identify churn-prone customers early.
