Restaurant Food Delivery Analysis

This project analyzes restaurant food delivery performance using Python, Pandas, NumPy and Matplotlib. The goal is to measure average delivery time, delayed orders, traffic impact, weather impact, vehicle type performance and delivery efficiency.

Project Overview

Food delivery companies need to deliver orders quickly and consistently. Delayed deliveries can reduce customer satisfaction and affect business performance.

This project analyzes delivery time, delayed orders, traffic density, weather conditions, vehicle type and other operational factors to understand delivery performance.

Business Problem

The main business problem is to identify the major reasons behind delayed food deliveries. This analysis helps understand how traffic, weather, delivery vehicle type and order patterns affect delivery time and service quality.

Key KPIs

Total Orders

Total food delivery orders analyzed in the dataset.

Average Delivery Time

Average time taken to deliver orders.

Delayed Orders

Total orders that took longer than expected.

Delay Rate

Percentage of delayed orders out of total orders.

Tools Used

Python Pandas NumPy Matplotlib Jupyter Notebook

Analysis Performed

  • Loaded and explored food delivery data using Pandas.
  • Checked rows, columns, data types and missing values.
  • Cleaned column names for easier analysis.
  • Cleaned delivery time column and converted it into numeric format.
  • Created delivery status segments such as Fast, Normal and Delayed.
  • Calculated total orders, average delivery time, delayed orders and delay rate.
  • Analyzed average delivery time by traffic density.
  • Analyzed weather impact on delivery performance.
  • Compared delivery time by vehicle type.
  • Created charts using Matplotlib.

Key Insights

  • Average delivery time helps measure overall delivery performance.
  • High traffic density increases average delivery time.
  • Bad weather conditions can increase delivery delays.
  • Vehicle type can affect delivery speed and operational efficiency.
  • Delay rate helps identify service quality and customer satisfaction issues.

Business Recommendations

  • Allocate more delivery partners during high traffic hours.
  • Use traffic and weather data to estimate delivery delays more accurately.
  • Improve route planning to reduce delivery time.
  • Monitor delayed orders regularly to improve customer satisfaction.
  • Use vehicle type performance insights for better delivery partner planning.
